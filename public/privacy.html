Record My Trip

Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This explains what data Record My Trip (the website at map.recordmytrip.com and the Record My Trip app for Android) collects, why, where it goes, and how to remove it. Short version: you can use the map and record trips without an account, and then everything stays on your phone. With an account, your trips are backed up privately and only become visible to others if you share them.

Who we are

Record My Trip, contact: privacy@recordmytrip.com. Write to us about anything in this policy, or to exercise any of your rights below.

Without an account

  • Your location is used on your device to show you on the map and to record trips. It is not sent to us.
  • Trips, photos, saved places and saved map areas are stored only in your browser or the app on your phone.
  • When you search, look up a place, get directions or view the map, your device asks the map services listed below directly; they receive your IP address and what you looked up (for example the area of the map you are viewing).

Sharing

  • Shared trips. Each trip is private until you choose Unlisted (anyone with the link) or Public (also listed in Explore). The start and end of a shared trip are hidden (500 m unless you change it), only the photos you tick are shown, with their location data removed, and only the date is shown, not the time of day. You can make a trip private again at any time.
  • Live sharing. While you share a trip live, anyone with the link sees your route, current position, speed and stats, except within 300 m of where the trip started. The link stops working when the trip ends, after the time you chose, or when you stop it, and the location data is then deleted. Ended links are removed after a day.
  • Views. To rank Explore, we count one view per person per trip per day, using a one-way code made from the viewer's IP address and browser. We don't store the address itself, and these codes are deleted after two days.
  • Reports. If you report a trip, we keep the reason and your account so we can review it.

Background location (the app)

While you are recording a trip, the Android app keeps using your location with the screen off, so your route has no gaps. You'll see a "Recording your trip" notification for as long as this happens. It stops when you finish the trip. The app does not use your location in the background at any other time, and asks only for "while using the app" permission.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking cookies. The only cookie is the one that keeps you signed in.

To see whether the app is useful, we count how it's used, anonymously: only the kind of thing that happened (for example "opened the app", "recorded a trip", "shared a trip") and the day. No locations, trip details, names or what you type. To count people rather than taps, your account (or, signed out, a random code made on your device) is turned into a code that changes every month, so you can't be followed from month to month. These counts are kept for about a year. Vercel Web Analytics also counts page visits, without cookies.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and backed-up data: until you delete them.
  • Live share locations: deleted as soon as the share ends.
  • View codes: two days.
  • Data on your device: until you remove it (or clear the app's or browser's data).

Your rights and deleting your data

  • Delete your account in the app or on the website: Account › Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and everything in it: trips and photos, shared and live links, saved places, and your public name. You can also email us to ask for deletion.
  • Get your data: export any trip as a GPX file (Trips › GPX), or email us for a copy of everything we hold.
  • Correct or restrict it: edit or make trips private yourself, or email us.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Record My Trip is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and say so in the app.
